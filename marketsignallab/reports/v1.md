# V1 experiment notes

## Scope

V1 is a deterministic, long-only research backtest. It uses dated rolling-universe
snapshots, randomly selects competing entry candidates, and never sends broker
orders. QQQ is a benchmark and cannot be traded.

## Execution assumptions

- Signals use information available at the daily close.
- Orders fill at the ticker's next available daily open with fractional shares.
- Overnight gaps are preserved and recorded as `gap_return`.
- No commissions, slippage, taxes, spreads, or liquidity impact are modeled.
- Missing feature history produces no entry signal.
- The research window begins on January 3, 2023.
- The portfolio supports five active positions targeting 20% each.
- Sidelined half-positions remain in portfolio equity but do not consume an active
  slot.

## Universe assumptions

- Alpha Vantage supplies listed-on-date rosters.
- `company_evidence.csv` supplies approximate technology classification.
- `tech_seed_universe.csv` supplies manual seed names and position classifications.
- The current evidence is not true historical discovery evidence and can introduce
  survivorship or lookahead bias.

## Evaluation questions

1. What return and drawdown distributions appear across random seeds?
2. What percentage of seeds beats QQQ?
3. Which tickers repeatedly create catastrophic half-sales or drawdowns?
4. How much capital becomes trapped in sidelined positions?
5. Do spike exits and controlled re-entries improve later outcomes?

These results are research output, not investment advice.
